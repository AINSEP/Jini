`@jini-ai/mcp/federation/hosted` exports `createPinnedCapabilityRuntime` (immutable pinning of a host-owned loopback capability with an exact-endpoint bearer supplier) and `startCapabilityDelivery` (cancellable delivery of a changing capability to a spawned child); the host injects the endpoint policy and environment names.
